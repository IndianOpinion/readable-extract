Live: counting day in the state assembly election

10:00

Results for the first twelve seats are expected before noon, the chief electoral officer told reporters at the press room.

9:00

Turnout figures released at nine put the final number just above sixty-eight per cent, slightly higher than last time.

8:00

The election office said postal ballots would be counted first in every centre before the machines are opened.

7:00

Early trends show a close contest in the northern districts, with three seats changing leads twice.

6:00

Counting began at eight in the morning at all forty centres across the state, officials said.
